# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Native wgpu AV recorder.
- H.264 and ProRes video recording.
- No-audio, microphone, and audio-file modes.
- CPAL microphone capture to temporary float WAV.
- ffprobe audio-file validation.
- Post-recording mux without video re-encoding.
- Persistent custom output folder.
- High-resolution capture diagnostics.
