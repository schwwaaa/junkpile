Voxel Feedback Volume

A persistent 3D RGBA16F storage texture evolves entirely on the GPU. Compute transports, diffuses, erodes, injects, and recursively preserves voxel history; a second pass raymarches the living volume as fog, slices, maximum-intensity projections, or thresholded surfaces.

Volume definition

Each voxel stores HDR color plus density. Memory grows cubically; two 256³ RGBA16F volumes use about 256 MiB before output and driver allocations.

Start at 128³. A higher voxel definition increases both compute dispatch cost and raymarch detail.
