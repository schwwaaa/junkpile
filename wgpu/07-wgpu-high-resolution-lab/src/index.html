High Resolution Lab

Render a procedural HDR frame into an offscreen target at window resolution, 1080p, 4K, or 8K, then downsample it to the native surface. This separates rendering definition from display-window size.

Internal resolution

8K RGBA16F is a serious allocation: one 7680×4320 target is about 253 MiB. Multipass or ping-pong designs multiply that cost. Select 8K deliberately and watch frame rate, thermals, and memory pressure.
