Volumetric Raymarcher

Construct animated three-dimensional worlds from signed-distance functions. Every output pixel casts a camera ray, advances through procedural space, estimates a surface normal, then adds lighting, shadows, ambient occlusion, fog, glow, HDR post-processing, and native high-resolution output.
